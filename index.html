      The MLP Mini — How to Play

      Fill the 5 × 5 grid using the Across and Down clues. Every answer shares letters with another answer.

      SELECTTap a square to begin.
      SWITCHTap the selected square again to change between Across and Down.
      TYPEEnter letters with the keyboard. Arrow keys move through the grid.
      CHECKFlags incorrect letters without revealing the answer.
      CLEARRestarts the current puzzle.
      FINISHComplete the grid to stop the clock and unlock Copy Result.
